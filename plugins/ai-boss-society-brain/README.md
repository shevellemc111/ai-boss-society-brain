# AI Boss Society Brain

The AI Boss Society Business Brain Builder kit, packaged as one plugin.

## What's inside

**Builder skills (do these first)**

| Skill | Builds | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| business-brain-builder | `business-brain.md`: the operating brain your AI employees run on | Required |
| writing-rules-builder | `writing-rules.md`: your voice, from three real writing samples | Required |
| about-me-builder | `about-me.md`: who you are as an operator | Required |
| brand-kit-builder | `brand-kit.md` plus your real letterhead file | Recommended |
| how-i-work-builder | `how-i-work.md`: standing rules for how Claude works with you | Optional |

**AI employees (skeletons, filled in live at the training)**

- business-ai-intake-coordinator
- business-ai-email-manager
- business-ai-operations-assistant

These three are marked `[FILL IN AT EVENT]`. Don't pre-fill them; we build them together using the STAR Framework.

## How to start

After installing, open Cowork and say: **"Let's do my business brain."**
